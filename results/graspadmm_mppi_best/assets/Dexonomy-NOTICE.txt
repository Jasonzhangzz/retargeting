This repository retains only HandObjMatcher, sample_init_pose,
HandTemplateLoader._load_data and their numerical/query utilities, plus the
Shadow annotated templates and kinematic XML needed for input initialization.
Python imports were relocated; the Warp device helper is called through its
current public API. Unused task solvers, filters, viewers and pipelines are removed.
Local initialization adaptations and their annotations are described in README.md.
