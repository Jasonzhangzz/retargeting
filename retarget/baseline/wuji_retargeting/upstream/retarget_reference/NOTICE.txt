Independent RetargetSession reconstruction for validation of wuji-sdk 2026.8.31.

The specialized symmetric eigendecomposition and Householder QR routines
are a Python/NumPy adaptation of nalgebra v0.33.2:
  https://github.com/dimforge/nalgebra/tree/v0.33.2
  src/linalg/symmetric_eigen.rs
  src/linalg/symmetric_tridiagonal.rs
  src/linalg/householder.rs
  src/linalg/givens.rs
  src/linalg/qr.rs
  src/geometry/reflection.rs
nalgebra's package author is Sebastien Crozet; it is licensed under Apache-2.0.
The upstream license is preserved in LICENSE-nalgebra.txt.
Adaptation changes: real 3x3 specialization, Python arrays/scalars, explicit
floating-point accumulation order for comparison against the pinned SDK,
and bounded diagnostic checks where described in the source.

Other reconstructed formulas and control flow are documented by the local
machine-code evidence and independent validation reports in the parent folder.
The four URDF files in assets/ were extracted byte-for-byte from the user's
installed SDK for this local analysis. Their origin, byte offsets and hashes
are recorded in the asset manifest; no new license is asserted for those files.
This package is not an official Wuji SDK release.
